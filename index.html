How I work

  1. Understand the task. We start with the work that takes your time: who asks, what they ask and where the information lives.
  2. Build the workflow. I build it in n8n and connect AI models to the tools you already use, such as Gmail, Google Sheets or Airtable.
  3. Test with real examples. I run it with realistic cases and fix what breaks before it reaches your customers.
  4. Hand it over. You receive the working workflow, a short walkthrough and the workflow file.
